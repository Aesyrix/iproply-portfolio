The brief

Make it look like the premium product it claims to be.

The platform worked, but the site undercut it. Each page had its own style, the colors were loud, and some of the most visible numbers had no data behind them.

  • No single look. Bright amber yellow, Bootstrap blue and rounded pills sat side by side, and the chat bubble was a stock blue circle.
  • Claims without data. "171.5 Million+ buyers", "98% satisfaction" and hard-coded "+16%" trend badges on the dashboard.
  • Agent blurbs shown as reviews. "Affiliated with X. Serves: Y" appeared as five-star customer testimonials.
  • Broken pieces. The Reviews page was blank, blog sidebar images were broken, and registration said "4 steps" for a 3-step form.
  • Heavy pages. The Contact hero image alone was 19.6 MB.

Design system

One palette, two typefaces, sharp corners.

Every page now draws from the same small set of choices. Deep navy for depth, champagne gold used sparingly for what matters, and ivory text that stays calm at night.

2px and 4px

Buttons have 2px corners and cards 4px. The old pills and 28px bubbles are gone, which reads as more architectural.

Gold means action

Gold marks the primary button, the active state and key figures. Everything else stays navy or ivory.

Serif for numbers

Prices and stats are set in Cormorant with lining figures, so "$1,791/mo" feels like a listing, not a spreadsheet.

New features

Built for people actually house-hunting.

Listings

Map search

Results sit beside a live map. Every match gets a navy price pin, and nearby homes group into numbered clusters.

  • Click a pin for a mini card with photo and price
  • Hover a card and its pin lights up gold
  • "Search this area" reruns the search after you pan

Property detail

Monthly payment estimate

Sliders for down payment and rate, plus a 15/20/30-year switch. The total updates live with a breakdown of principal, tax, insurance and HOA.

  • Same amortisation formula as the full calculator
  • "Ask about financing" goes straight to the agent

Engagement

Saved homes

A heart on every listing, a counter in the header, and a shortlist page. It works without an account.

  • "Ask an agent about these" opens a pre-filled inquiry
  • A browser becomes a qualified lead

Comfort

Dark mode

A moon button switches the whole site to deep navy with ivory text. The choice is remembered and applied before the page paints, so there is no white flash.

  • Map tiles, chat widget and forms follow along
  • A contrast audit over 32 pages found 0 unreadable text items

Every surface

Sign-in to dashboard, one language.

The redesign didn't stop at the marketing pages. Agents and admins see the same system from the moment they sign in.

Honesty pass

Removing what couldn't be backed up.

A premium brand loses trust fastest when a visitor checks a number and it doesn't hold. Every claim on the site was traced to its source, and anything invented was replaced.
